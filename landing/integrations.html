Integrations

Connect what you already use

JustCall

What connects
Call recordings.
How
Paste your API key and secret from your JustCall dashboard — no OAuth flow. This is JustCall’s own supported method.
What happens next
Closed calls are pulled automatically, transcribed, and scored.

Intercom

What connects
Conversations and Tickets.
How
OAuth — click Connect, approve, done.
What happens next
Closed conversations and tickets are pulled automatically (webhook-driven, with a polling backstop), including screenshots and internal notes. A case that touches both a conversation and a linked ticket is merged into one audited case automatically, not scored twice.

Credentials are stored in a dedicated, encrypted vault — never shown again in the UI once saved, never logged.
